Service

What Hi Silver AI does

It does not diagnose, and it does not act on your behalf.It gathers and organizes records and provides guidance so that you can make your own decisions.

1

Medical visit preparation

Summarizes recent changes in medication, tests and symptoms on a single page to show at medical visits.

2

Insurance claim preparation

Gathers medical bill receipts, itemized statements and medical certificates, checks them against your insurance policies, and points out the items that can be claimed.

3

Presenile stage · Dementia preparation

Records changes in daily life and guides you to where you can be screened.
Helps prepare a digital legacy list, a dementia-care trust and guardianship consultations.

4

Caregiver connection

Within the scope you have consented to, sends caregivers summaries of check-ins, medication and medical appointments.

5

Putting affairs in order

Organizes digital assets and stores them in the vault, and prepares access by designated persons.

6

After-death preparation · Funeral

Sets in advance the emergency contact order, funeral arrangements and the people to contact.
If check-ins stop, it checks step by step and provides guidance on funeral and after-death procedures.

Check-ins · Daily life

Holds scheduled check-in conversations.Notifies when something differs from usual.

Medication · Health records

Reminds you when to take medication.Organizes blood pressure and blood sugar records into a table.

Target users

It starts with individuals and families.It will expand to hospitals, care facilities and public institutions.

No diagnosis

It does not make judgments about illnesses or readings.It advises you to check with a doctor or pharmacist.

Warning sign verification · Prevention

It verifies warning signsand works out prompt preventive/preparatory measures.

Does not act on your behalf

It does not handle legal, financial or administrative matters on your behalf.It guides you through procedures and documents.

Records stay on your device

Health and financial records are kept in the vault on the device.Originals are not sent to a server.

Your consent comes first

Only what you consent to is shared with caregivers.Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

AI only guides

Answers generated by AI are labeled as such.You make the decisions.
