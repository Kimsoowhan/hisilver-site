Service

What Hi Silver AI does

It does not judge·diagnose, and it does not intervene.By the user's decisions and choices, it gathers and organizes records, and guides/supports.

1

Medical visit preparation

Summarizes·provides medication·test·symptom changes as documents needed for medical care.

2

Insurance claim preparation

Gathers medical bills, itemized statements and diagnoses, checks them against your insurance contracts, and then points out the items eligible for claims.

3

Presenile stage and dementia preparation

Records changes in daily life and guides you to where you can get screened.
Helps prepare a digital legacy list, a dementia safety trust and guardianship consultations.

4

Caregiver connection

Within the scope you consent to, sends caregivers a summary of check-ins, medication and appointments.

5

Putting affairs in order

Organizes digital assets, stores them in the vault, and prepares access by designated persons.
Guides you in advance to designate a phone legacy contact (Samsung, Apple).

6

After-death preparation · Funeral

Sets in advance the emergency contact order, funeral arrangements and the people to contact.
If check-ins stop, it checks step by step and takes care of the funeral, the aftermath and the family.

Check-ins · Daily life

Holds scheduled check-in conversations.Notifies when something differs from usual.

Medication · Health records

Reminds you of medication times.Organizes blood pressure and blood sugar records into tables.

Who it serves

It starts with you and your family.It will expand to hospitals, care facilities and public institutions.

A day with Hi Silver AI

A day of the agent

Not a list of features: this is how it works every day.

Morning

Check-in and medication

Holds a check-in conversation and announces medication time. Records responses and whether medication was taken.

Daytime

Medical visit and schedule preparation

Checks hospital appointments and schedules, and makes a one-page summary to show at the visit.

Evening

Record keeping · Caregiver summary

Organizes blood pressure, blood sugar and medication records into tables, and summarizes only the consented scope for caregivers.

When there is no response

Step-by-step checks

Contact again → Caregiver check → Verify warning signs and work out prompt preventive/preparatory measures.
It protects lives through urgent crisis response (109↔112) linked with emergency and rescue services.

To the end

Funeral · After-death arrangements

Following the wishes set in advance, it notifies the designated people and cares for the funeral and the family’s well-being afterwards.
