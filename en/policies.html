2. Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-24 · Applies to beta versions of HIVEN AI agents

1. Controller

The current controller is the representative (founder of HIVEN, in incorporation). After HIVEN is incorporated, control transfers to HIVEN with prior notice on this page and in the app.

2. Processing in the app

There is no sign-up. All processing (file organizing, vault) happens only on the user's PC and nothing is sent to the internet automatically.

3. Purpose and items (error/feedback reports, optional consent)

Purpose: fixing errors and improving the beta and HIVE-Group AI agents. Items: app version, OS version, error code, step, comments written by the user, reply email (if provided). Basis: the user's optional consent (all features work without it).

Reports are collected only when the user previews the content and presses 'Send'. Vault contents, file names/paths, passwords and recovery QR are never included.

4. Collection channel

A web form managed by HIVEN; until it opens, email (contact@hiven.co.kr).

5. Retention and destruction

Kept during the beta and destroyed without delay (within 5 days) after the beta ends; electronic files are deleted irrecoverably.

6. Third parties

Not provided to third parties.

Users may withdraw consent or request access, correction, deletion or suspension at any time via contact@hiven.co.kr; consent can also be withdrawn in the app.

Access is limited to the operator, storage media are encrypted and processing is logged.
